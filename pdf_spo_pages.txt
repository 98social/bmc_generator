Abstract
A Study on the Business Model Canvas of Social  Purpose 
Organizations(SPOs): Focusing on the case of Korea Etiquette 
and Cultural Center(KEI)
 Jin Sook, Choi*/Jae Hee, Chun**
 
  SPOs are pursuing social and economic values as an intermediate type of 
organization between profit and non-profit. Therefore, in order to achieve the 
dual purpose of SPOs, a business model canvas reflecting the characteristics of 
SPOs is required. However, research on SPOs business model canvas is very 
scarce. Therefore, the purpose of this study is to explore the applicability of 
the business model canvas for social purpose organizations(SPOs). In this 
study, the meaning of the business model canvas of SPOs and SPOs was 
clarified through a review of previous studies. In addition, as a canvas suitable 
for SPOs, the business model canvas for social enterprises proposed by 
Qastharin(2014) was proposed, and the suitability and practicality of the 
business model canvas were analyzed through the success case analysis of the 
Korean Etiquette Institute(KEI). As a result of the case analysis, it was 
reviewed that the canvas is suitable for use by SPOs with a dual purpose of 
creating social and economic values and is highly practical. First, through KEI 
analysis, it was confirmed that a differentiated dual value proposal consistent 
with SPOs??mission was necessary and possible by distinguishing between 
co-producers and beneficiaries in the block in the customer segment. These 
results imply that a business model including stakeholders is required to 
maintain the permanence of a social purpose organization, and components of 
mission and impact measurement must be included to confirm the organization?셲
